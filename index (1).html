Controls and tips
  • Keyboard defaults: arrow keys to move, X / Z / A / S for buttons, Enter for Start, Shift for Select. Gamepads work too.
  • Use the bar under the game to save states, rebind keys, go fullscreen, or turn on cheats.
  • N64 and DS games run heavy. Chrome or Edge on a decent computer works best.
